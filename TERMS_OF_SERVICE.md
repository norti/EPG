## 2. Description of the Software

EPG Viewer is a free, open-source desktop application for viewing Electronic Program Guide (EPG) TV schedules from user-provided XML/GZ sources.

- EPG Viewer is **not** an IPTV provider, media player, or video streaming service. It does not host, bundle, distribute, or provide television streams, channels, or copyrighted broadcast content.
- Optional integrations allow you to enrich programme metadata via TMDB, connect your Trakt.tv account, back up/restore your settings using your own Google Drive `appDataFolder`, and add TV programme reminders to your own Google Calendar.
